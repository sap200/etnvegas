Instructions

Introduction

Welcome to the ETN Vegas Gambling App! This guide will help you navigate through the app and place your bets effectively. Follow these step-by-step instructions to get started and make the most out of your gaming experience.

Backing Up Your Account

Your account security is paramount. The app does not store your private key, so it's crucial that you keep it in a safe place. If you lose your private key, you will lose access to your funds permanently, with no way to recover them.

To back up your private key:

  • Go to the "Profile" section.
  • Click on "Private Key".
  • Tap "Copy" to copy the private key to your clipboard.
  • Store it in a secure location, such as a password manager or offline storage.

Depositing Funds

To buy chips, you need to deposit Ether into your account. Follow these steps to deposit Ether:

  • Go to the "Wallet" section.
  • You will see a "Receive" button. Click on it to reveal your wallet address.
  • Send Ether to your wallet address to deposit funds.

Buying Chips

You can buy chips from the "Shop" section of the app. Here are some available options:

  • 5 Chips
  • 20 Chips
  • 100 Chips

If none of these options meet your needs, you can select the "Customized" option, where you can specify the exact number of chips you want to purchase.

Withdrawing Your Winnings

ETN Vegas ensures that you loose or receive chips, just like in any casino. To withdraw your winnings, follow these steps:

  • Go to the "Wallet" section and click on the "Swap" button.
  • Clicking "Swap" will exchange your chips for Ether.
  • The Ether you receive can be sent to any address by using the "Send" button in your wallet.

Slot Game

The slot game in Etn Vegas is intuitive and easy to play. To play the game:

  • Enter your desired bet amount.
  • Click on the "Play" button to spin the slot.
  • Based on the result, your winnings (if any) will be automatically credited to your account as chips.
  • Your chip balance will be updated immediately. You can then swap these chips for Ether if desired.
